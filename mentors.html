Questions About Mentorship

Which departments have a mentor?

We aim to have an experienced industry mentor for every department on set. Right now our mentors cover producing, directing, camera, art, post-production and legal, and we’re adding more each season. Each mentor’s card shows their department. Where a department doesn’t have its own mentor yet, students still get guidance from the mentors on set, who work across every part of a production.

Do mentors make the movies for us?

No. Students hold every role on all four movies. Mentors work beside you in your department, answering questions and stepping in with advice when a scene gets tricky.

When do mentors get involved?

From the start of pre-production on May 3 through to wrap on August 31: helping plan the shoot, on set for all four movies, and in the edit suite through final delivery.

Do I need movie experience to learn from a mentor?

No. You don’t need to study film to join the crew, and mentors are there to help you learn the role. See who can apply.

Will mentors help with my career after the summer?

Mentors share how they built their careers and offer advice on reels, credits and next steps, so you leave with more than four movies on your résumé.
