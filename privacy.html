Privacy Policy

Buzzflix Inc. runs this website. This page explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how to ask us to see, correct or delete it.

When you apply

The application form asks for your name, email address, school, program and year, previous experience, why you want to join and up to three positions you’d like. You can also attach a resume and portfolio as PDF files, which we keep in a private Google Drive folder with your application. We also record when you applied and which version of the application terms you agreed to.

We use this to assess your application and to contact you about it. Only the Buzzflix team reviews applications. We don’t sell your information or share it with advertisers.

Applications are stored in a Google spreadsheet through Google Apps Script, so Google stores them on our behalf. They may be stored outside Canada.

When you save your answers before applications open

If you use “Email me my answers”, we store your answers and email address with a random code, then email you a link that brings your answers back. The email holds only the link, never your answers. We delete saved drafts after applications close.

How long we keep it

We keep applications with no set end date, so we can consider you for future seasons and keep a record of who has applied. You can ask us to delete yours at any time (see Your choices below). Saved drafts are the exception: we delete them after applications close.

Buzz Run

If you add your score to the Buzz Run top 10, we store the three letters you choose, your score and when you set it. Pick initials rather than anything that identifies you. Your best score and initials are also saved in your own browser so the game remembers them.

Visiting the site

The site is hosted by Cloudflare, which processes your IP address to deliver pages and protect the site from attacks. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. It doesn’t use cookies or track you across other websites.

The site uses no cookies. Your browser keeps a few settings on your device, such as whether you closed the announcement bar or turned off the bee cursor. You can clear them in your browser settings at any time.

YouTube videos load from youtube-nocookie.com only when you press play. The map of Windsor loads from OpenStreetMap. Both services receive your IP address when they load, and their own privacy policies apply.

Your choices

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted, including withdrawing an application. Email info@buzzflix.ca and we’ll reply within 30 days.

If you aren’t satisfied with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update it here and change the date at the top of the page.
