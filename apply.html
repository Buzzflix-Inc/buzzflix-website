Frequently Asked Questions

Who can apply?

Current students and recent graduates from any program who are Canadian citizens or residents. You don’t need to study film or live in Windsor, and past crew are welcome to apply again.

When and how do I apply?

Applications for summer 2027 open on January 18, 2027 and close in March 2027. Feel free to include a link to a reel or portfolio; creative submissions are always appreciated. If you’re shortlisted, we’ll invite you to an online interview.

Will I be paid?

Yes, all student crew earn a flat rate of $250 per day, whatever their role.

When does the summer run, and what are the hours?

Pre-production starts in Windsor on May 3, and we wrap on August 31: four months to make four films. Like most film sets, the days are long. Expect 12-hour days, five days a week.

Are housing and transportation provided?

No. You’ll need to arrange your own housing in Windsor. We shoot around Windsor and in neighbouring towns, and crew members often carpool to set.

Which films and departments will I work on?

You’ll work on all four films and be credited on each one. If you’re hired in one role, you can apply for a different department on the next film, and there are post-production roles on all four.

Will there be mentors on set?

Yes. Industry veterans from Hollywood and the Canadian film industry mentor students in various departments. Learn more about our mentors.

Can I post about my work on social media?

Yes! Unlike most productions, Buzzflix encourages everyone to document and share their experience on their own social channels, linking back to Buzzflix’s YouTube and social platforms.

Where can I watch the films?

On our YouTube channel. You can also find past productions on our Movies page.
