Superstar

A socially awkward teen’s world is transformed when the most popular girl in school asks him out, but as envy and rivalry threaten his fragile place among the “cool clique”, he must decide whether the price of acceptance is worth losing who he truly is.

Be a Boss

Just the Facts

After losing his spot on the basketball team to an arrogant newcomer, a jealous teen hacks into his rival’s livestream to spread a humiliating rumour. What starts as a comedic revenge stunt quickly spirals out of control when he discovers his target is secretly battling a life-threatening illness.

Questions About the Movies

Who makes the movies?

Students write and develop the scripts before the summer starts, then students and recent graduates hold every role on all four movies, from directing and camera to art and post-production. Industry veterans mentor them along the way. Meet our mentors.

Where can I watch the movies?

In Canada, the movies are broadcast on Hollywood Suite. Internationally, they’re distributed by A71 Entertainment Inc. Every episode of the docu-series is on our YouTube channel.

Where are the movies shot?

In and around Windsor, Ontario, including neighbouring towns, at real locations like Walkerville Collegiate.

How long does it take to make four movies?

Each movie gets one week (five days) of pre-production, then shoots for between 14 and 17 days. The crew makes all four movies in one summer, from the start of pre-production in May to wrap at the end of August.

What is the docu-series?

Our crew films everything that happens while making the movies, and edits it into episodes on our YouTube channel. The episodes cover every part of the filmmaking process, from casting to post-production, and are turned into a feature-length documentary at the end of the summer.

Can I work on the next movies?

Yes. Applications for summer 2027 open on January 18, 2027. See how to apply.
