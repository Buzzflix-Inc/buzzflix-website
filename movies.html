Summer 2026 Productions

Superstar

A socially awkward teen’s world is transformed when the most popular girl in school asks him out, but as envy and rivalry threaten his fragile place among the “cool clique”, he must decide whether the price of acceptance is worth losing who he truly is.

Behind the scenes: Superstar docu-series episodes

Be a Boss

Forced to confront his father’s declining health and his family’s financial crisis, a carefree teen abandons the easy path of social media and popularity, risking friendships and status to build a business that could save his family—and redefine his future.

Behind the scenes: Be a Boss docu-series episodes

Just the Facts

After losing his spot on the basketball team to an arrogant newcomer, a jealous teen spreads a false rumor online to ruin him—only to discover his target is secretly battling a life-threatening illness, forcing him to confront the consequences of his lie.

Behind the scenes: Just the Facts docu-series episodes

OFF-LINE

A popular senior shocks her friends by giving up her phone for a 30-day digital detox—only to discover that going offline exposes hidden truths, fractures her curated life, and forces her to decide whether disconnecting is freeing her… or costing her everything.

Behind the scenes: OFF-LINE docu-series episodes
