Our Goal

Buzzflix is a student-driven film initiative designed to create high-quality, digital-native films while providing paid, real-world experience to emerging filmmakers. Our goal is to remove the financial and structural barriers that prevent young creatives from gaining meaningful industry experience and to replace them with opportunity, equity, and mentorship.
